Worked as a product management intern in PCARDS for 1 month. Despite being an intern, I was given the opportunity to closely work with the Director of PCARDS, which was quite a valuable experience. He guided me closely as a mentor and helped me understand the whole thought process of the product development cycle. The work consisted of both practical and research-based activities. Overall, the whole process was very helpful for my personal development in terms of building and learning the practical thought process behind product development while developing soft skills on the side, such as sales, UI/UX, task organization, networking etc.
