Business Development Executive

Job summary

Are you someone who loves building connections, exploring new opportunities, and helping businesses grow? We’re looking for a dynamic Business Development Executive to join our team. In this role, you’ll be responsible for finding new business prospects, making deals, and building strong relationships with clients.

Eligibility:

  • Bachelor’s degree in any stream (Business, Marketing, or a related field is preferred).
  • Strong communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively in a team.
  • Results-oriented with a track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify potential clients and industries that align with our products.
  • Contribute to lead generation efforts through online research, networking, events, conference.
  • Understand customer requirements.
  • Present them the product effectively.
  • Ability to explain complex information clearly and simply.
  • Good sales and negotiation skills.
  • An honest and trustworthy manner.
  • Resolve customer complaints quickly and effectively.
  • Building and maintaining profitable relationships with key customers.
  • Overseeing the relationship with customers handled by your team.
  • Keeping customers updated on our products in order to increase sales.

Expectations

  • Experience in sales domain is preferred.
  • Maintain a positive attitude focused on customer satisfaction.
  • Problem-solving attitude.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Teamwork and leadership skills.
  • Customer-oriented mindset.
