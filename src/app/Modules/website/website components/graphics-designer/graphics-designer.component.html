Graphics Designer

  • Full-Time
  • Nagpur

Job summary:

We are looking for a talented and creative Graphic Designer to join our team. As a Graphic Designer, you will play a key role in shaping our visual identity and enhancing the overall aesthetic appeal of our brand.

Qualifications:

  • Proficiency in Graphics, Graphic Design, Product Mock-ups, and Branding
  • Ability to create creative and striking visual contents for multiple platforms and purposes
  • Proficiency in design tools such as Adobe Creative Suite or Figma.
  • Experience in logo design and able to produce a logo design timely and on-brand
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively, meet tight deadlines, and prioritize workload effectively
  • Knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite- Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, and CorelDraw are a must
  • Ability to take constructive feedback and iterate designs rapidly
  • Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design or equivalent experience is preferred
  • A portfolio showcasing the applicant’s best work is required

Responsibilities:

Design Development:

  • Create visually engaging graphics for digital and print media.
  • Collaborate with teams to deliver effective visual solutions.

Branding:

  • Ensure brand guideline adherence for consistent design elements.
  • Contribute to the evolution of our brand identity.

Collaboration:

  • Work with cross-functional teams to understand project objectives.
  • Iterate on designs based on feedback for a positive team environment.

Deadline Management:

  • Manage multiple design projects simultaneously.
  • Prioritize tasks efficiently to meet project deadlines.
